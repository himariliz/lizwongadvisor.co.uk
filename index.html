My Story

Where Strategic Thinking Meets Human Understanding

I came to the UK as an educator and entrepreneur — and that journey taught me something crucial: financial decisions aren't just about numbers. They're about the life you're building and the future you're protecting.

As an FCA-regulated mortgage and protection adviser, I work with first-time buyers, business owners, seasoned investors, and expats buying UK property with overseas income. What they all have in common? They want someone who thinks strategically but speaks like a human being — someone who explains the why, not just the what. Understanding how the UK system actually works is far more important than most people think, and as a former senior educator, I make sure you actually understand your options before you decide.

Multilingual, with a particular understanding of BNO familiesI'm fluent in English and Cantonese, and fair in Mandarin. I welcome clients across the UK via Zoom, with a particular insight into the journey faced by BNO families, Hong Kong newcomers, and expats buying with overseas income — but my door is open to clients from every background.

Strategic. Empathetic. Real.
