My Journey

Not Stepping Back — Stepping Up

I qualified in mortgages and protection (CeMAP and CPSP) and spent time working as an adviser — helping clients navigate one of the biggest financial decisions of their lives. I loved the work. But the deeper I went into the industry, the more I realised something: the questions my clients really needed answered went far beyond mortgages.

How does UK tax actually work? What happens to your pension if you move here from Hong Kong? Why do so many people get protection wrong? How does the whole system fit together? These weren't side questions — they were the questions. And I wanted to be able to answer them properly.

"I'm not leaving mortgages and protection behind — I'm building on them. Studying toward full IFA qualification is the natural next step for someone who genuinely wants to understand the whole picture, not just one part of it."

While I complete my IFA studies, I'm channelling everything I know into education — breaking down the UK financial system in plain English and Cantonese, so that first-time buyers, business owners, investors, and newcomers to the UK can make genuinely informed decisions. As a former senior educator with 18 years of teaching experience, explaining complex things clearly is something I've always done.

Strategic. Empathetic. Real.
